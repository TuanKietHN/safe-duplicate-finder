## Constitution Check

*GATE: Must pass before Phase 0 research. Re-check after Phase 1 design.*

- **Data preservation**: Default flow is scan-only/dry-run; no source deletion; at least one verified
  copy remains per duplicate group.
- **Evidence**: Strict duplicate proof and pre-mutation stability checks are explicitly designed.
- **Transactions**: Journal ordering, same-volume quarantine, restore, and crash reconciliation are
  specified with failure boundaries.
- **Tests first**: Safety tests and fault injection precede mutation implementation.
- **Bounded/local processing**: Streaming, backpressure, memory/resource budgets, local-only privacy,
  and structured audit logging are accounted for.
- **Safety case**: Data-loss hazards, mitigations, residual risks, and verification evidence are linked.

Any failed gate MUST stop planning unless the constitution permits a documented, time-bounded waiver.
